# Rewired v0.8.3

Rewired v0.8.3 makes big chips like PIX6 much lighter to look at, and adds an experimental GPU renderer.

## Big chips are lighter on the CPU

Zoomed out on a chip with thousands of parts (PIX6 has 1565 NAND gates and 3326 wires), most names, pins and wire bends are smaller than a pixel, yet they were still drawn every frame. Now:

- chip names and pin labels are not drawn when they would be smaller than 3 pixels;
- pins are not drawn when they are smaller than a pixel;
- wire bends closer together than a pixel are drawn as one straight segment;
- a per-frame search that grew with the square of the number of parts is gone.

Zoomed in, everything looks the same as before.

## Experimental: GPU rendering

**Settings → Rendering** has a new choice, **GPU (BETA)**. The wires and chips of the circuit you are viewing are sent to the graphics card once and stay there, and each frame only the wires whose signal changed are updated. Zooming and panning cost almost nothing.

While you select, move, place or edit something, the standard renderer is used, and the GPU copy is rebuilt when you are done. It is off by default and not available in the browser. If something looks wrong, switch back to **STANDARD**.

## Editor

- The parts panel animates when a section opens or closes, and on touch screens the highlight glides to the selected category.
- New projects start with the grid shown and snapping set to *If Grid Shown*. Existing projects keep their settings.

## Compatibility

- Projects are unchanged; v0.8.2 opens projects saved by this version.
- **Android**: v0.8.2 installs this version from the app with *UPDATE*.
